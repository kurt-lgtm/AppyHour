- **CSV path fix** — writes to `InventoryReorder/fulfillment_web/` instead of `GelPackCalculator/` (per domain alignment — fulfillment results belong with fulfillment).
- **Dry-run response** — now includes `target_counts` summary and `rc_bundle_only` flag for audit.
